# NOX v4.13.39 — Premium Navigation & Connected Service Journeys

## Navigation
- Simplified the primary labels to Residential, Commercial, Fire & Compliance and Service & Monitoring.
- Reworked desktop dropdowns from large cards into compact service rows with a separate Support & next steps column.
- Reduced dropdown height and visual weight while keeping one relevant project or system image.

## Customer journeys
- Fire alarm installation now links directly to servicing, monitoring, compliance packages and emergency lighting.
- Emergency lighting now links directly to lighting servicing, fire alarm servicing, monitoring and maintenance packages.
- Intruder alarm pages now connect monitoring, maintenance, CCTV and perimeter protection.
- CCTV pages now connect intruder detection, perimeter protection, CCTV maintenance and integrated site security.
- Home CCTV and perimeter pages now connect into monitoring and ongoing support.
- Commercial CCTV, intruder, industrial, perimeter, multi-site and integrated pages now each offer four connected next steps.
- Monitoring and maintenance-plan pages now link into installation, takeovers, compliance, multi-site and integrated support where relevant.
- Related-service grids can now show four routes rather than stopping at three.
