# NOX Website v4.1 — Ajax product imagery and positioning

## Scope

- Standardised Ajax product artwork across the intruder, perimeter, CCTV and fire product sections.
- Replaced mixed white-canvas product images with consistent dark product canvases.
- Preserved full device proportions with `object-fit: contain` rather than zooming or cropping products.
- Kept real installation photography on `object-fit: cover`, with consistent central positioning.
- Added responsive product-image ratios for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- No page URLs, SEO metadata or page structures were changed.
