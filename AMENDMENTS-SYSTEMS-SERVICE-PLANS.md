# NOX v2.6 — Systems and Service-Plan Refinement

## Navigation
- Added a clear Home link to desktop navigation.
- Removed Perimeter Protection from the main navigation.
- Rebuilt Systems navigation around Residential Installations and Commercial Installations.
- Rebuilt Service Plans around Residential Aftercare and Commercial / Fire Support.

## System design
- Residential routes now cover Ajax intruder alarms, residential CCTV and smart-home CCTV for ordinary family homes through to larger and rural properties.

- New installations are kept separate from monitoring, maintenance and compliance packages.

## Ajax presentation
- Replaced red-background product graphics with cleaner official Ajax product imagery or real NOX installation photography.
- Expanded MotionCam, KeyPad TouchScreen, DoorProtect, Ajax NVR and DoorBell descriptions.

## Service plans
- Preserved every confirmed annual price.
- Pages without an approved fixed price now state that pricing is quoted to the system and site.
- Residential and commercial packages are displayed separately and consistently.
- Fire alarm servicing, emergency-lighting servicing and compliance packages are positioned as ongoing commercial support.

## Footer and conversion
- Updated footer routes to match the new residential/commercial structure.
- Removed the Perimeter Protection footer link.
- Added direct Fire Alarm Installation, Emergency Lighting and Fire Risk Assessment links.
