# NOX v4.13.49 — Modern homepage visual refinement

Baseline: v4.13.48 fire-first service priority.

- Replaced the generic camera hero with the genuine NOX property/van image.
- Introduced a darker cinematic hero treatment with stronger typography and cleaner rounded CTAs.
- Kept fire-first messaging in the H1 and lead copy.
- Added a prominent four-card service priority grid immediately below the hero:
  1. Fire Alarms
  2. Commercial CCTV
  3. Home CCTV
  4. Home Intruder
- Added a compact premium proof rail for installation, compliance, maintenance, monitoring and customer type coverage.
- Refined the existing NOX delivery journey, service cards and supporting panels with softer radii, improved image proportions and cleaner spacing.

- Updated homepage metadata/social image to the genuine NOX property/van image.
- Preserved the existing fire-first navigation and deeper service hierarchy from v4.13.48.

## Responsive behaviour
- Four priority cards collapse to two columns on mobile while retaining service order.
- Hero typography, CTA layout, proof rail and local coverage panel have dedicated mobile treatments.
- Images use object-fit cover and responsive object positioning rather than stretching.
