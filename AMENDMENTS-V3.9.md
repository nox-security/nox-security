# NOX Website v3.9 — Editorial Simplification

## Purpose

This release keeps the established black-and-white NOX design and existing route structure while removing repetition, internal website-strategy language and unclear overlaps between installation products and annual support.

## Homepage

- Reduced the homepage length and removed the repeated Core Systems, full Guides, full Areas and repeated ongoing-support sections.
- Kept a clear hierarchy in the hero:
  - Primary: Get a Quote
  - Secondary: Call and WhatsApp
  - Supporting routes: Home Security, Commercial Security, Fire & Compliance and Existing Systems
- Retained prominent Residential, Fire & Compliance and Commercial sections.
- Retained selected genuine NOX projects, the Why NOX section, two relevant reviews and one final enquiry section.
- Removed repetitive explanations already covered by the main service hubs.

## Navigation

- Preserved the established navigation structure.
- Removed the duplicate Service Plans link from the Servicing & Monitoring dropdown.
- Kept the overview as the dropdown heading and retained direct links to servicing, monitoring, maintenance, takeovers, repairs and the Total Security Plan.

## Residential Security Packages

- Kept the existing URL to protect internal links and indexing history.
- Repositioned the page as a new installation package rather than an annual service plan.
- Separated the installation quotation from optional monitoring and maintenance.
- Changed the process to property survey, specification, installation and handover.
- Removed annual-renewal and annual-pricing language from the installation presentation.

## Reviews and trust

- Replaced repeated three-review blocks with service-relevant proof.
- Homepage: one residential and one commercial review.
- Residential and residential systems: residential Ajax review.
- Commercial pages: commercial CCTV review.
- Fire pages: commercial Fire & Intruder review.
- Servicing pages: aftercare-focused review.
- Reviews mentioning previous team members remain available on the dedicated Reviews page but are not repeated across current service pages.
- Updated visible review proof to 46+ Google reviews.
- Added Residential, Commercial, Fire and Servicing variants of the trust strip.
- Clarified that Fire risk assessments and extinguisher servicing are coordinated through competent specialists where required.

## Contact and Get a Quote

### Get a Quote
- Retains the GoHighLevel Website Enquiry form.
- Reduced surrounding copy.
- Added concise trust proof, next-step explanation and direct Call/WhatsApp alternatives.

### Contact
- Removed the duplicated enquiry form.
- Focuses on telephone, WhatsApp, email, opening hours, service area and existing-customer/fault guidance.
- Includes a clear button to the main Get a Quote page.

## Customer-facing wording

- Removed visible references to SEO strategy, conversion routes, search relevance, thin content, keyword lists and invented outcomes.
- Rewrote those passages around the customer, property, service scope and practical next step.
- Reduced repeated use of internal terms such as “route” where normal customer language was clearer.

## Fire-service clarity

- Fire Alarm Installation remains focused on new systems and replacement projects.
- Fire Alarm Servicing remains focused on planned testing, records, defects and recurring support.
- Fire repairs and takeovers remain focused on faults, access, system condition and supportability.
- Fire risk assessments and extinguisher servicing now state the competent-specialist arrangement clearly.

## Existing rankings and URLs

- No existing service URL was changed.
- `/systems/intrusion-alarms` was not substantially rewritten or restructured.
- Existing canonical, sitemap and redirect structures were preserved.
