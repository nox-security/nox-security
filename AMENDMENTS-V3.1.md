# NOX Website v3.1 – Clarity and Conversion Refinement

This update preserves the v3.0 visual foundation while simplifying navigation and the homepage.

## Navigation
- Added a less literal Systems structure based on core systems and property routes.
- Restored Perimeter Protection to the main Systems navigation.
- Removed pricing from all navigation dropdowns.
- Reduced the Service Plans dropdown to six clear support routes.
- Removed duplicate residential/commercial maintenance links from the navigation.

## Homepage
- Shortened the homepage and removed repeated sections.
- Kept the residential and commercial distinction without repeating full installation menus.
- Introduced three primary system routes: Intruder, CCTV and Fire.
- Linked Perimeter Protection naturally from the Intruder route.
- Removed the duplicate second case-study grid, homepage blog feed, local-search block and video showcase.
- Reduced the Ajax product rail from six items to four larger product cards.
- Combined ongoing support into Alarm, CCTV and Fire/Compliance routes.

## Systems and Service Plans
- Added Perimeter Protection to the main Systems page.
- Reframed the Systems page around core systems first, followed by residential and commercial context.
- Unified residential and commercial plan cards on the Service Plans page.
- Kept pricing on the relevant pages, with residential and commercial prices shown consistently inside each plan card.
- Retained dedicated detail pages for every service and package.

## Product imagery
- Enlarged Ajax product imagery on the homepage.
- Increased product-image areas across system pages.
- Changed product grids to two columns on desktop for clearer, larger product presentation.
