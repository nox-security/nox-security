# NOX Fire & Security — Navigation & Customer Journey Report (V4.13.29)

## Executive summary

The website has been reorganised around what the visitor is trying to do: protect a home, protect a business, manage fire compliance or get help with an existing system. Existing URLs and content remain in place; the change is primarily navigation, overview-page hierarchy, internal routing, CTA clarity and mobile behaviour. No service page was deleted.

## 1. Final navigation structure

- **Protect My Home** → `/residential`
  - Home CCTV
  - Ajax Intruder Alarms
  - Perimeter Protection
  - Smart Security
  - Support for an Existing Home System
- **Protect My Business** → `/commercial`
  - **Installations:** Commercial CCTV; Commercial Intruder Alarms; Industrial & Office Security
  - **Larger Projects:** Multi-Site Security; Integrated Fire & Security
- **Fire & Compliance** → `/fire-compliance`
  - **Fire Alarm Systems:** Installation; Servicing; Repairs & Takeovers; Multi-Site Fire Alarm Systems
  - **Emergency Lighting:** Installation; Testing & Servicing
  - **Compliance:** Fire Risk Assessments; Fire Extinguisher Servicing; Fire Compliance Plans
  - **Integrated Projects:** Fire & CCTV Integration
- **Existing System Support** → `/service-plans`
  - Servicing & Maintenance
  - Monitoring
  - System Takeovers
  - Repairs & Upgrades
  - Ongoing Support Packages
- **Projects** → `/case-studies`
- **About** → `/about-us`
- **Get a Quote** → `/get-quote#quote-form`

The desktop headings remain clickable overview links while hover reveals direct service routes. On mobile, the first tap opens the service choices and the overview remains available as a smaller link inside the accordion.

## 10. Final customer journey review

1. A visitor lands on the homepage, a local landing page, a project or a guide.
2. The page identifies whether the requirement is residential, commercial, fire/compliance or an existing system.
3. The visitor can enter the overview for guidance or go directly to the relevant specialist service.
4. The service page explains the requirement, scope, process, project proof, local relevance and next action.
5. The CTA carries the visitor to the quotation form with contextual enquiry information where available.
6. GoHighLevel receives the enquiry and redirects to `/thank-you`.
7. GA4 records the completed enquiry as `generate_lead`, while calls, WhatsApp and quote clicks remain separate intent events.
8. The enquiry can then move into survey, quotation, installation and the relevant ongoing support route.
