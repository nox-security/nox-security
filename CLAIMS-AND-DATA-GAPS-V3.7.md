# NOX Website v3.7 — Claims Removed, Verification Limits and Genuine Content Gaps

## Claims and wording removed or deliberately not introduced

- No police-response claim was introduced.
- No URN claim was introduced.
- No SSAIB, NSI or NACOSS approval claim was introduced.
- No Grade 3 installation-capability claim was introduced.
- No Farsight reference was introduced.
- No free-installation claim was introduced.
- No guaranteed response-time or emergency-callout claim was introduced.
- No unsupported insurer-recognition claim was introduced.
- No unsupported aggregate-rating structured data was added.
- A legacy `Grade 3 anti-snap lock replacement` service-plan item was replaced because it was not evidenced as a current NOX offering.
- Universal `Request Your Free Security Survey` wording was removed in favour of service-relevant or general quote actions.
- Monitoring copy explicitly distinguishes app alerts from professional monitoring and avoids implying guaranteed attendance or police response.

## Data not available for this implementation

The following could not be reviewed because access or exports were not supplied:

- Google Search Console query/page data for three, six and twelve months.
- Search Console device split, CTR opportunities and cannibalisation reports.
- Backlink data.
- Google Analytics conversion-path data.
- GoHighLevel custom-field IDs and workflow mapping rules.

The form now passes useful query-string context, but final mapping into named GoHighLevel contact/opportunity fields requires the relevant field IDs or confirmation that the embedded form recognises those parameters.
