## Consolidation notes

- `/systems/cctv` remains the broad CCTV solutions hub. `/systems/home-cctv` and `/commercial/cctv` serve distinct residential and commercial buying intent.
- `/systems/fire-safety` serves installation intent. `/service-plans/fire-alarm-servicing` serves maintenance intent. `/services/fire-alarm-repairs-takeovers` serves fault and takeover intent.
- `/systems/emergency-lighting` serves installation intent. `/service-plans/emergency-lighting-servicing` serves testing and maintenance intent.
- Package pages remain conversion routes and are not treated as the primary organic-search strategy.
- Location-focused `/services/*` routes remain in place where they already exist. New town-swapped duplicates were not created.
