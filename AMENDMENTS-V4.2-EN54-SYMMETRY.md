# NOX Website v4.2 — Ajax EN54 and layout symmetry

## Scope

This revision builds on v4.1 without changing the established navigation, URLs, visual identity or overall page structure.

## Fire & Compliance overview

- Expanded the Fire & Compliance overview with a dedicated Ajax EN54 section.
- Added balanced cards covering:
  - EN54 Fire Hub
  - Addressable smoke and heat detection
  - Manual call points, sounders and visual alarms
  - Range extension and I/O options for larger premises
- Kept Ajax EN54 positioned as one suitable commercial fire platform rather than implying that it is right for every building.
- Retained conventional and wired addressable alternatives.
- Reorganised the overview into a symmetrical two-card installation section and six-card ongoing-compliance section.
- Added a direct link to the Telesis Hitachi EN54 project.

## Fire Alarm Installation page

- Improved the page around the complete fire-system design rather than isolated products.
- Added clearer coverage of control equipment, zones, detection, manual activation, sounders, VADs, communications, standby power, commissioning and future servicing.
- Added a six-card product/system section with consistent image framing.
- Clarified that Ajax EN54 can support compatible intrusion, automation and video devices on suitable projects while the fire-alarm scope remains clearly defined.
- Kept conventional and wired addressable systems visible as alternatives.

## Overview wording

Restored the preferred v3.9 hero title and introductory wording on:

- Residential
- Fire & Compliance
- Commercial Security
- Servicing & Monitoring

SEO metadata from the newer version remains in place, so the customer-facing wording is more natural without weakening the latest search targeting.

## Symmetry and spacing

- Added equal-height card rows across system, project, product, guide and review grids.
- Standardised common image ratios and card-body spacing.
- Balanced Fire installation cards into two equal columns.
- Balanced Ajax EN54 highlights into four equal columns.
- Balanced the Fire support section into two rows of three cards.
- Added responsive two-column and single-column layouts for tablets and mobiles.
- Kept product artwork contained and centred while real installation photography remains naturally cropped.
