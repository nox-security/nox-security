# NOX Fire & Security — Premium Interaction Refinement

**Release:** V4.13.30  
**Base:** V4.13.29 Premium Navigation & Customer Journey  
**Purpose:** Refine the finished website without redesigning it or changing the established customer journey.

## 1. Refinement principle

The black-and-white NOX design, navigation structure, service routes, page content depth, case studies, analytics and conversion setup have been retained. This release concentrates on the shared interaction system so the website feels calmer, more deliberate and easier to use across desktop and mobile.

No service page was removed. No established URL was renamed. Existing redirects remain available.

## 2. Homepage hierarchy

The homepage continues to lead with the three primary services:

- Fire Alarms
- CCTV
- Intruder Systems

The main heading remains **Fire Alarms, CCTV & Intruder Systems**. The opening copy now makes the commercial and premium-residential positioning clearer and reinforces the core service area across Chesterfield, Sheffield, Derbyshire, Derby and the Peak District.

The first customer routes remain:

- Protect My Home
- Protect My Business
- Fire Alarms & Compliance
- Existing System Support

These routes have been visually simplified so the page guides visitors without adding another layer of explanation or choices.

## 3. Desktop navigation

The customer-journey structure from V4.13.29 is unchanged. The dropdown presentation has been refined through:

- more balanced panel widths and spacing
- clearer service-group hierarchy
- restrained typography and labels
- a softer layered black surface with controlled blur and shadow
- reduced visual borders around individual service links
- smoother entrance and hover movement
- quieter active-page indicators
- service links shown before the overview route
- overview links moved into a lower-priority position at the bottom of each panel
- replacement of the previous arrow treatment with a subtle animated line cue

The result keeps individual revenue-generating services as the primary choices while preserving the SEO and guidance value of every overview page.

## 4. Mobile navigation

Mobile has been treated as a complete experience rather than a reduced desktop menu. Improvements include:

- a full-height, focused navigation panel
- a controlled dark backdrop
- opening and closing animations
- body-scroll locking while the menu is open
- Escape-key closing on supported keyboards
- clearer accordion spacing and hierarchy
- plus/minus-style section feedback instead of traditional arrows
- service links presented before overview links
- a persistent contextual action at the end of the menu
- safe-area spacing for modern mobile devices
- improved focus visibility and reduced-motion support

The menu remains structured around the same four customer situations and does not force visitors through an overview page before showing individual services.

## 5. Context-aware enquiry actions

The header and persistent mobile conversion bar now adapt to the visitor's current route while continuing to use the same enquiry form and analytics setup.

Examples include:

- **Arrange a Fire Survey** on fire and compliance routes
- **Book a Commercial Survey** on commercial routes
- **Book a CCTV Site Survey** on the main CCTV route
- **Book a Home Security Survey** on residential routes
- **Request a Service Quote** on maintenance routes
- **Request a Monitoring Quote** on monitoring routes
- **Discuss a System Takeover** on takeover routes
- **Get Support for Your System** on repair and upgrade routes
- **Discuss a Similar Project** on case studies
- **Book an Ajax System Survey** on the Ajax partner page
- **Book a Site Survey** as the calm default route

Query parameters preserve the service and enquiry context when the visitor reaches the quotation form. Existing GA4 click and lead tracking remains intact.

## 6. Buttons and interaction system

The shared button system has been refined rather than restyled page by page. Changes include:

- more consistent pill geometry
- calmer hover lift and press feedback
- controlled shadow and contrast
- consistent animation timing
- improved keyboard focus states
- clearer spacing between primary and secondary actions
- replacement of generic labels with specific survey, project, service and support wording

The update affects shared button components across the website while retaining the established NOX visual language.

## 7. Ongoing support and recurring revenue

Monitoring, servicing, maintenance and future upgrades are now introduced more naturally after installation content. They are positioned as the next stage of owning a professionally installed system rather than as unrelated products.

Fire alarm monitoring has not been added as a primary navigation item. Fire servicing pages can explain suitable signalling and ongoing support naturally. The main Monitoring route within Existing System Support remains focused on intruder alarm monitoring, keyholder communication and maintained systems.

## 8. Internal journey and page protection

The following route families remain connected through overview pages, filters, footer navigation, related links and shared conversion sections:

- residential system pages
- commercial security pages
- fire and compliance pages
- existing-system support pages
- all service landing pages
- all project case studies
- all guides
- all retained location pages

Dynamic project and guide hubs render the complete case-study and article collections. The service directory renders the complete local-service collection. The areas hub links all 31 established location pages, with core growth areas presented first and legacy locations retained in a deliberately low-prominence continuity section.

No missing internal route targets or missing image references were found in the automated audit.

## 9. Location focus

Primary visible positioning now concentrates on:

- Chesterfield
- Derbyshire
- Derby
- South and West Sheffield
- The Peak District

Higher-priority local corridors include:

- Dore and Totley
- Ecclesall, Fulwood and Ranmoor

Rotherham, Barnsley and Worksop are not promoted within the primary navigation, homepage, footer or principal location journey. Established location URLs remain live where needed to avoid discarding previous SEO value.

## 12. Final customer journey

1. A visitor immediately understands that NOX specialises in Fire Alarms, CCTV and Intruder Systems.
2. They choose whether they need home protection, business protection, fire and compliance work or support for an existing system.
3. The dropdown presents the most useful individual services first.
4. The chosen page answers that specific requirement and links to relevant projects, guides, locations and support options.
5. The persistent action changes to match the page and carries the correct context into the quotation form.
6. A successful GoHighLevel enquiry redirects to the private thank-you page and records a genuine `generate_lead` event.
7. Monitoring, servicing, maintenance and upgrades are introduced naturally as the long-term continuation of the installation.

The finished experience remains recognisably NOX, but the interaction system is calmer, more consistent and more commercially intentional.
